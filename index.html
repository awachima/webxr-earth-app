How to use Meetup Rooms
×

Enjoy the same VR tour at the same time

Meetup Rooms let you and your friends watch the same DokodemoDoors VR tour at the same time, even if you are far apart.

1. Create a room

Click the “Create Meetup Room” button, set a title, meeting time, max participants, and paste the destination URL of the tour you want to visit together.

2. Share the link

After creating the room, share the URL of the lobby page with everyone who will join. They can access it from a PC, smartphone, or VR headset browser.

3. Enter the lobby and start the tour

When the meeting time comes, click “Enter Lobby”. From there, everyone can jump to the same destination URL and enjoy the VR tour at the same timing while talking together.
