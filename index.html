What is Dokodemo Doors?
×

A place where you can explore the world together, even when you're far apart

Dokodemo Doors is an online VR platform that offers a wide collection of 360-degree VR travel videos from around the world.

More than 1,700 videos available

Although YouTube also has VR content, no other site gathers this many VR travel videos in one place.

Watch together in perfect sync

By starting the video at the same time, you can share the exact same scenery while talking or using gestures.

What is this fan site?
×

An unofficial fan-made site that lets you choose destinations from a 3D globe

I created this site with official permission because I wanted to pick destinations visually on a globe instead of from a traditional list view on the official site.

1. Subscription required

Viewing each world requires a subscription through the official website. If you're not subscribed, entering a world will redirect you to the subscription page.
Some worlds are available for free during limited periods, and those are easiest to find from the official homepage under “Recommended Worlds.”

2. Meta Quest 2 or higher recommended

Although the service works on various devices, the VR experience is overwhelmingly superior. For best results, Meta Quest 2 or higher is recommended.
